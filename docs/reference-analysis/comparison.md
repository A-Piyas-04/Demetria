# Reference Comparison — Kemin × Eurofeed × XVET

## Strongest design elements

| Area | Strongest reference | Why |
|------|---------------------|-----|
| Visual premium / hero | **Kemin** | Full-bleed science photography, clear hierarchy, polished corporate feel |
| Content IA / technical sections | **Eurofeed** | Outcome-based product categories, R&D, certifications, services |
| Product catalog UX | **XVET** | Group + species + solution filters, product cards, inquiry CTAs |
| Credibility storytelling | **Kemin** | Metrics strip + science narrative structure |
| Contact persistence | **Eurofeed** | Always-reachable phone/email patterns |
| Testimonials / field proof | **XVET** | Structured testimonial layout (use placeholders only) |

## Homepage layout suitability

- **Best base:** Kemin-style hero → category grid → about → quality → CTA
- **Inject:** Eurofeed technical category naming
- **Inject:** XVET featured product cards + inquiry CTA

## Simplest navigation

XVET’s concise primary destinations + one strong CTA outperform Kemin’s enterprise mega-nav and Eurofeed’s dense WordPress menus.

**Chosen model:** Home · About · Products · Solutions · Research & Quality · Insights · Contact (+ “Request inquiry” CTA)

## Product presentation

XVET wins for browse/filter/discover. Eurofeed wins for category taxonomy language. Combine: XVET interaction + Eurofeed category semantics.

## Shared sections (duplicated purpose)

All three offer: hero, products/solutions, about/science story, contact. Merge into one set of sections — do not triplicate.

## Unnecessary features to exclude

- Region/language selectors (Kemin)
- Unrelated industry divisions (Kemin textiles/food)
- Dense certification logo walls with unverifiable claims
- Shopping cart / accounts
- Live chat
- Campaign microsites

## Deeper crawl confirmation (follow-up)

Extended page walks of Eurofeed (R&D, Background, Certifications, GI Health) and XVET (catalog, Health group, PDP, Contact, Company) confirmed the initial split:

- Keep **Kemin-like** visual premium and hero photography seriousness (avoid Eurofeed’s playful carousel tone).
- Keep **Eurofeed** trust/R&D/certification IA — not its hamburger-only desktop nav or text-only SKU dumps.
- Keep **XVET** three-axis discovery and card metadata — add what XVET lacked: product-level inquiry CTA + visible desktop nav.
