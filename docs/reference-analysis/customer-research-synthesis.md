# Customer Research Synthesis — Demetria (Placeholder Brand)

**Mode:** Lightweight synthesis from public industry language and reference-site IA (not interviews).  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Rule:** Observed facts vs inference labeled. No fabricated customers.

## Observed (public sector / reference sites)

- Buyers browse by **outcome** (gut health, pathogen control, feed quality) and by **species**.
- Trust content often includes R&D narrative, certifications presentation, and field/application stories.
- Conversion patterns: product inquiry, distributor inquiry, technical consultation.
- Pain themes repeated across animal nutrition B2B sites: diet transitions, mycotoxins, feed efficiency, antibiotic reduction pressure.

## Inference (for copy direction only)

| Buyer worry | Copy implication |
|-------------|------------------|
| Will this survive pelleting / water application? | Mention format, heat, dosing language as placeholders |
| Can we audit the program? | Emphasize documentation slots, COA, technical onboarding |
| Is this one more additive SKU? | Lead with pathway + related products, not feature dumps |
| Who owns implementation? | Promise partnership language (mill + farm), not “set and forget” |

## Messaging pillars for enriched content

1. **Outcome pathways** before chemistry class  
2. **Species context** on every product card  
3. **Technical partnership** as the product, not just the bag  
4. **Honest placeholders** where proof is missing  

## Gaps requiring client input

- Real ICP interviews, sales-call mining, regional regs, approved claims, certification list
