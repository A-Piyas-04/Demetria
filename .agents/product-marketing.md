## Product

**Working name:** Demetria Nutrition  
**Category:** B2B animal nutrition — feed additives, premixes, nutraceuticals, mycotoxin risk tools  
**What we sell:** Specifiable formulations + technical partnership for mills, integrators, and producers  
**What we do not claim yet:** Verified certifications, peer-reviewed outcomes, named customer logos

## ICP

### Primary buyers
- Nutritionists and technical managers at feed mills / integrators
- Procurement leads evaluating additive suppliers
- Distributors seeking a partner brand for regional coverage

### Secondary
- Farm / production managers (influence specs; rarely sole buyers)
- Quality / regulatory staff (need documentation packages)

### Jobs to be done
1. Stabilize gut performance through diet transitions and stress windows
2. Manage pathogen and mycotoxin risk without vague “natural” claims
3. Improve nutrient capture from corn-soy and alternative ingredients
4. Document programs for audits and antibiotic-reduction roadmaps
5. Onboard a supplier that can train mill and farm teams

## Positioning

**For** feed mills and animal protein producers who need measurable nutrition programs,  
**Demetria Nutrition** is a technical feed-additive partner  
**that** connects species-specific formulations to outcome pathways (gut health, feed quality, efficiency, farm KPIs)  
**unlike** broad chemical catalogs or opaque “black box” blends,  
**we** lead with clear pathway framing, replaceable technical specs, and consultation-first inquiry.

## Customer language (research-informed)

**Observed industry phrasing (public B2B sites / sector vernacular — not client interviews):**
- “diet transition,” “gut integrity,” “pathogen pressure,” “mycotoxin risk,” “feed hygiene”
- “less antibiotics / NAGP,” “FCR,” “water application,” “pelleting stability”
- “technical service,” “COA,” “inclusion rate,” “species-specific”

**Inference (not observed VOC):** Buyers distrust unsupported performance numbers and want dosing + documentation more than brand poetry.

## Brand voice

- Precise, calm, scientific — not playful
- Benefit-led, species-aware, falsifiable where possible
- Always label samples / placeholders until client proof lands
- Avoid AI tells and empty superlatives (“best-in-class,” “revolutionary”)
